01 — About

Bridging mechanics and computation

I'm an MSc student in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at North South University (NSU), Dhaka, where I also work as a Graduate Teaching and Research Assistant. My background bridges mechanical engineering and applied mathematics: a BSc in Mechanical Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), followed by graduate coursework in PDEs, numerical methods, and high-performance computing.

My research centers on computational fluid dynamics and heat transfer — most recently, pulsatile flow around tandem elliptic cylinders in power-law fluids — approached through both classical finite-volume/finite-element solvers and physics-informed neural networks. I'm drawn to problems where data-driven methods and first-principles physics have to work together, particularly in turbulence modeling and reduced-order flow representation.

I'm currently preparing PhD applications in Mechanical Engineering, with a particular interest in turbulence closure, wall-modeled LES, and data-driven approaches to complex flows.
